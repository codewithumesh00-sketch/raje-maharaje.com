# Raje Maharaje — Luxury Sartorial & Heritage Atelier

A high-performance luxury e-commerce experience designed with the refined aesthetic of the **Shopify Gravity Theme**, celebrating the monumental craft lineage of Indian royalty (*Kings & Queens, Pure Banarasi Zari Silk, Awadhi Chikankari, Royal Bandhgalas, Velvet Achkans, and Bespoke Heirloom Presentation Chests*).

---

## 👑 Features & Sections

- **Editorial 4K Hero Lookbook**: Dynamic 2.0s model showcase with marquee typography and responsive CTA.
- **`EXPLORE // CATEGORY`**: 6-grid responsive category tiles with bottom-left dark frosted glass labels.
- **`EXPLORE // COLLECTION`**: 5 tall portrait lookbook cards with `Sold out` badge styling.
- **Dark Staggered Category Showcase**: Deep charcoal background with staggered cards and white typography.
- **`TRENDING // NOW`**: 8-product grid with exact discount badges (`14% OFF`, `16% OFF`, `Sold out`, etc.), quick action cart buttons, and frosted title/price bars.
- **`FEATURED // SIGNATURE PRODUCT` Quick Buy**: 2x2 zoom gallery with full-screen lightbox, color swatches with active rings, size selectors, quantity stepper, and the vibrant Cyan Blue (`#0084B4`) `Buy it now` button.
- **Royal Atelier Video Craftsmanship Showcase**: High-definition video player with royal silk and zari footage, play/pause, mute toggle, and playlist switcher.
- **Interactive Bespoke Box Builder**: Real-time 3-piece curated royal gift chest builder with live 3D preview and molten wax seal personalization.
- **Sartorial Pocket Square Folding Guide**: Step-by-step interactive folding guide for Presidential, Royal Crown, Sovereign Puff, and Winged folds.
- **Slide-out Cart & Wishlist Drawers**: Instant drawer checkout with automatic free shipping calculations and promo code support.
- **Global Currency Switcher & Precision Formatting**: Clean `Rs. X,XXX.00` pricing format.
